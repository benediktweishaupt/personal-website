Each printed matter of the "Graduation Festival 2014" is based on a Wordpress blog. Starting from the website of this project, posters, banners, flyers and an approx. 200-page exhibition catalog were generated, which was printed on demand on site. Students were able to update images of their final project during the festival and visitors could take away an up-to-date printed version of the catalog as a unique copy each day.
