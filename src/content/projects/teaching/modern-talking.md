During the two-week course, students learned the basics of programming using the text editor Atom, implementing HTML, JS and CSS. During a five-day field trip to transmediale, students populated an Instagram account whose content was fed into a dynamic poster generator. The course concluded with a workshop supervised by Hackers & Designers.
