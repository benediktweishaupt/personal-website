Quantification and the algorithmic processes associated with it are the central characteristic of an increasingly technically mediated time. In this project we dealt with technologies that raise algorithmic thinking to a new level: "Artificial Intelligence". The Rijksmuseum's extensive digital image archive served as a starting point for us to draw parallels between historical museology and artificial intelligence.
