"The Invisible Cities" by Italo Calvino is a mosaic of 55 portraits of cities. Based on selected cities of the book, full-spherical 360° illustrations for smartphones / tablet computers were developed, which interpret and narrate the respective city with animations, minimal interaction and sound. We worked in Unity.
