The participants of the project chose a place to make the subject of their investigation. The places could be represented in their function, in their meaning as a possibility for social processes, in their poetic and emotional dimension, in their spatial and temporal structure. Exhibited in the Kunsthaus Bethanien under the name of "Sichtweisen".
