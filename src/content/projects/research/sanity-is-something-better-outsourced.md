Sanity is Something Better Outsourced was created during a residency in Chemnitz, an east-german city at the time running up to become European Capital of Culture. In this context we dealt with the city's wishes for revaluation, while exploring its instrumentalization by right-wing intellectuals: In particular the diffuse relationship-systems used in academic publications concerned with right-wing ideologies of cleanliness and "creative destruction". In three performances we dedicate ourselves to a radical right-wing movement that flirts with academic rhetoric.
