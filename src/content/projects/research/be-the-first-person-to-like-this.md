Youtube reported, that in 2015 users uploaded 400 hours of content every minute to their platform. That adds up to 65 years of content every day. Amongst those video a fight for attention, clicks, view, recommendations, reactions and comments is fought. Every human and bot is participating and paying with views. "Be the first person to like this" composes new videos out of videos nobody ever reached browsing youtube. Zero-view-videos. An undiscovered area by humans and algorithms.
