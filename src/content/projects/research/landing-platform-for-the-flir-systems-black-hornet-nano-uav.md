Plaster piece on nail developed for the group show "Nailing it: A ten finger exhibition on future tackles". The Black Hornet is a tiny helicopter-shaped drone produced for both consumer and military market. It uses photographical AI for image production. During Brussels Gallery Weekend it propels image operations, always touching base on the artists' middle-fingernail.
