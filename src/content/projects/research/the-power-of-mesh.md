Starting from the ideal of a decentralized alternative internet based on "wireless networks", "The Power of Mesh" deals with companies, states and NGOs that use this infrastructure for their own programs. During the "Festival du Graphisme Chaumont", a local mesh network presented a non-linear story that showed the interdependencies between DIY activists, data collectors, cultural colonists, hardware providers and decentralization advocates. Using a smartphone, people could log into various hotspots on the festival grounds. A poster installation provided additional information about the five actors.
