Figure of Teach is a participatory performance in which the audience physically experiences a diagram of the right-wing intellectual Jordan Peterson. In this meditation we feel into the dichotomies of order and chaos, of masculinity and femininity that he postulates. Participants are given the opportunity to express themselves on white panels leaning on the gallery walls. After the performance the chaos is cleaned up by a robot vacuum cleaner and the painted panels are hung up for presentation in the gallery.
