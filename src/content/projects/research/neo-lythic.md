This is the first experiment in using Youtube in a new way of algorithmic story telling. The videos of this series are all found footage movies. Completely selected and montaged by an algorithm. This movie investigates the reoccurring terms from the Neolithic age today in the context of new software. We crawl, scrape, dig, carve and mine again using numbers instead of stones. This project analyses the descriptive vocabulary of the neolithic and applies it to contemporary technology. It uses both pools of videos to montage a dialog between both areas of technology.
